He gets power by sucking light from Angels, and taking Sefirot away. He's an experiment that Lucifer never expected would reach the Throne of God and Universe Beyond.

He takes Khamael's Sefirot in the aftermath of the Tartarus Arc, where imbues himself with the Darkness in an attempt to fully judge the trio. He takes Raphael's in a deal and then Michael when he fails to kill him, even after returning to a pure Light form.

Satan reaches the Throne by transmuting Darkness into Light, through portal. He attacks the Heavens. Everyone, who thought Michael would win, assemble to the Throme but Satan launches a torrent of Darkness with obscures everything and prevents entry to the verse. "Because my God, the King of Kings, Lord of All Hosts and, Father will guarantee us victory." "Which God? Am I not greater than Jehovah?". Then the spirit of God within Adam (lookalike) appears before the true body. It's a system that prevents anyone without permission entering. "Everything that's happened is according to God's plan", "You took the Sefirah because I allowed you to. You were born through my will. Your failure here will proceed as I deem it nessecary." He reflecfs the Darkness and takes the Sefirah back, releasing the powers back to their holders in a light of holy flames. He leaves after. normal Angels cannot enter the verse as the Ohr Light will consume them. The Sefirot then gang up and fight, however an earlier attack by Gallitsur activates which temporarily makes Satan material and bleed. He decides to exit, however an attack by Sandalphon is knocked hitting the firmament allowing some of his Darkness to escape into the universe. All is well - time to rebuild but all Angels question Metatron and who he is as he and them don't know him - except Gallitsur, Lucifer, and Sandalphon.
